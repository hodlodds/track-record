# Forward-test follow-up: October 30, 2026

**Descriptive look: no pass/fail.** It does not change the October 20 decision (model **BF**, push alerts **enabled**).

Over 13 days and 2.84 million predictions on 38,823 coins, BF's odds matched what followed: crashes ran at 1.04 times the predicted rate, all nine probability bands were within tolerance, and every check on the model passed again. 72.0% of its HIGH RISK alert episodes were followed by a crash within the hour. The weakest days were the last two, October 28 and 29. 98.4% of scheduled scoring runs completed, against a 99% tolerance: 60 runs were missed during planned maintenance and a network outage.

This look was pre-registered as descriptive. Amendment 1, written on 2026-10-17 before any forward outcome was inspected, moved the binding decision to October 20; this look and the November 15 report are still produced, but they no longer decide. The evaluator cannot unseal the outcomes before 2026-10-30 01:55 UTC; it ran at 08:03 UTC. It is the October 20 evaluator with two changes: the window's end, and a reader for minute bars older than seven days, which the live table no longer holds; those come from the day archive, through the same filter. Pre-registration, manifest and timestamps: [`prereg/`](../prereg/).

Every result below is set against the October 20 tolerances.

## Coverage and timing

- **Coverage:** a horizon needed every market-wide minute bar. Every candidate was labelled except 17 on October 23 (no reference bar) and 6,038 on October 26, where one market-wide minute was missing.
- **Minute bars:** October 16 – 22 came from the day archive, October 23 onward from the live table, through the same filter. Reading the live table, the October 20 look labelled fewer horizons of the shared days (16,897 of 53,501 on October 16; 92 – 96% on later days), so those days have more rows here.
- **Issuance delay** (issued_at − t, one value per scoring run): 99th percentile 23.1 s, maximum 50.3 s.
- **Errors:** none. No row failed its hash, and every logged probability matched the frozen calibration maps.
- **Skill table:** on 177,226 of 2,849,204 logged rows (6.2%), the skill table in use was dated a different UTC day than the landmark; none was missing. A diagnostic, with no tolerance.

## Limitations
- Thirteen days still cannot establish temporal robustness or any trading benefit.
- The window contains the 3.2 days that decided on October 20, so it is not independent of that decision.
- The historical periods used for the quality gates were already seen.
- No threshold, model or calibration was changed in the window.
- These are product tolerances, not significance tests.
