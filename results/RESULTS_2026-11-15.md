# Forward-test report: November 15, 2026

**Descriptive report: no pass/fail.** It changes no served model and no threshold.

Over 29 days and 5.40 million predictions on 59,856 coins, the frozen BF's odds stayed close to what followed, though less closely than on October 30: crashes ran at 1.08 times the predicted rate (1.04 on October 30), all nine probability bands were within their October 20 tolerances, and 68.9% of its HIGH RISK alert episodes were followed by a crash within the hour. 46.2% of crash episodes were alerted at least 5 minutes ahead. By week, crashes ran at 0.98 times the predicted rate in the first week and at 1.08 – 1.15 in the later ones, unevenly: on November 9 at 1.64 and on November 13 at 1.37, outside the 0.80 – 1.25 range the October 20 rule sets for a whole window, while on November 4 – 8 the ratio was between 0.90 and 1.01. 99.1% of scheduled scoring runs completed.

This is the last report `PREREG.md` schedules. Amendment 1 (2026-10-17) moved the binding decision to October 20; Amendment 2 (2026-11-02) made this report descriptive, because the refit of BF served from November 2 read labels of October 15 – 31, which overlap this window. The report is produced with the registered metrics, from the forward log of the frozen B and frozen BF, which were scored on the same live inputs throughout, with their hashes unchanged. One registered item is missing: the 7-day block interval of the paired AP (see "Backup model B"). The evaluator cannot unseal the outcomes before 2026-11-15 01:00 UTC; it ran at 10:07 UTC. It is the October 30 evaluator with three changes: the window's end, the decision step (with its manual-audit check) removed, and a record of the filled minutes added. Pre-registration, amendments, manifest and timestamps: [`prereg/`](../prereg/).

The odds the API and the site served came from the frozen BF until 2026-11-02 22:41 UTC, from the refit `crashrisk-bf-2` until 2026-11-08 09:18 UTC and from the frozen BF again since, from November 2 on with the daily calibration guard G3 ([`prereg/SERVED_MODELS.md`](../prereg/SERVED_MODELS.md)). This report scores the frozen BF and B without G3, so on some days it differs from what the site showed.

Every result below is set against the October 20 tolerances, for comparison only.

## Coverage and timing

- **Coverage:** a horizon needed every market-wide minute bar. Every candidate was labelled except 17 on October 23 (no reference bar), 6,038 on October 26 (one market-wide minute missing) and 4,833 on November 3 (two minutes missing): 10,888 of 5,414,414 in all (0.2%).
- **Minute bars:** October 16 – November 7 came from the day archive, November 8 onward from the live table, through the same filter. Before labelling, minutes the main feed had missed were filled in its tables (the counts below are of minutes holding filled rows, before the filter). By hand, on October 23 – 25: 101 minutes of October 16 – 23 and 2 of October 24 from a second recorder of the same market data, and the outage hours of October 23 (01:18 – 06:34 UTC) rebuilt from the feed's own archive. Automatically from that second recorder, from October 25: 77 minutes in 33 runs. On October 28 the fill rule was narrowed to rows of pump-suffix coins on their own graduation pool, and the rows outside it were taken out of the fills made since October 25: the fills of 22 minutes (20 automatic, 2 by hand) were taken out whole and are not counted above; the others kept their remaining rows. The 14 minutes whose absence left 73,192 horizons of October 16 – 19 unlabelled in the October 20 decision are among those filled by hand, so those days are fully labelled here, as in the October 30 look. The bars were used as they are, as in the October 30 look.
- **Issuance delay** (issued_at − t, one value per scoring run): 99th percentile 22.8 s, maximum 86.2 s (October 31, 21:10 UTC).
- **Errors:** none. No row failed its hash, and every logged probability matched the frozen calibration maps.
- **Skill table:** the forward log also records which day's wallet-skill table the main feed had loaded, read from the feed's log by the scoring service. That record is incomplete. On 554,767 rows (10.2%) it is dated an earlier UTC day than the landmark: 167,751 on October 22, when it missed that day's load line (most of the 177,226 rows the October 30 look reported); 372,410 from November 6 to November 8, 09:15 UTC, after a machine restart on November 5 stopped the read returning lines and the record kept its November 5 value; and 14,606 on the 00:00 landmark of other days. From November 8, 09:20 UTC, after the scoring service restarted, it is empty (983,189 rows, 18.2% of the window's logged rows). The feed's own log shows each day's table loaded within five minutes of 00:00 UTC on every day of the window. The models do not read this record; they read the skill shares from the feed's minute rows. A diagnostic, with no tolerance.

## Limitations
- Descriptive (Amendment 2): no pass/fail and no model choice follows from these numbers.
- The window contains the days that decided on October 20 and the days of the October 30 look. The refit `crashrisk-bf-2` read labels of October 15 – 31; the weekly gate of Amendment 3 with origin November 7 compared its candidate with the frozen BF, with G3, on October 31 – November 6; and G3 reads the last three days' crashes each day. So outcomes inside the window were used before this report, and the window is not independent of those decisions.
- Before November 15 the site showed live outcome counts of the model it served: an hour-by-hour record, a 24-hour record by band and a 24-hour list of crashes. From November 8 that model was the frozen BF, with G3. Internal checks of the site read these counts on November 5 and 7. The numbers here come from the pre-registered computation alone.
- No threshold, model or calibration of the frozen B and BF was changed in the window.
- One 29-day window of one market; it cannot establish temporal robustness or any trading benefit.
- These are product tolerances, not significance tests.
